# Calibrate the Panel

Read `references/calibration-standard.md` completely.

This is the step that turns the panel from an opinion into an instrument, and it
is the step every agency skips. It costs about ten minutes, forty-five days
after a mailing.

## 1. Match the real outcome

Find the completed run and the outcome measure and horizon recorded in
`PANEL-BRIEF.md` BEFORE the drop. Use that one. Substituting a more flattering
number after seeing the result is not calibration.

Typical measures for a book-roll panel:

- inbound calls or emails that reference the letter, counted by the front desk;
- non-renewals within the mailed cohort against the same cohort last term;
- quote requests or declarations pages received, for a cross-sell letter;
- how many of the calls were the kind the office wanted versus angry ones.

That last one matters and is easy to lose: a letter that triples call volume has
not necessarily worked.

## 2. Append, never rewrite

Add one row to `calibration/CALIBRATION.md` with the run ID, the predicted
decision, the panel score, the actual outcome, the cohort, the elapsed horizon,
and notes. Leave the run packet and the panel snapshot untouched.

## 3. Diagnose a miss

Classify it as one or more of:

- a shape missing from the roster — someone in the book nobody modeled;
- stale or thin evidence behind a dossier;
- seats too similar to each other;
- synthesis error, where the reactions were right and the read of them was not;
- weighting error;
- something outside the letter entirely — the drop date, a carrier notice that
  arrived the same week, a competitor's mailing, the news;
- an outcome counted too early or too noisily to mean anything.

That sixth category is the honest one and gets skipped most. A renewal letter
that lands the same week the carrier's own notice arrives is not being read in
the conditions the panel assumed.

Do not blame a dossier merely because reality disagreed once.

## 4. Tune conservatively

Never change weights from a single result. After several comparable mailings,
propose the smallest evidence-backed adjustment and record why. Keep the old
manifest with the runs it governed.

Report calibration as this agency's observed history on this kind of letter.
Never as an accuracy rate, and never as a number that transfers to another
agency or another kind of mailing.
