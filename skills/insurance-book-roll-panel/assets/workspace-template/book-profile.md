# Book profile

This is the file the panel is built from, and the kit will not build seats
without it. Everything below is a question about YOUR agency. Answer in plain
sentences — rough numbers are fine, invented ones are not. If you do not know
something, write that you do not know it rather than filling in a figure.

The refusal gate (`scripts/check-letter-input.ts`) checks that this file has
real answers in it before any letter is read back through the book. It is not
checking your prose; it is checking that somebody actually filled it in.

## Client shapes we see most

Name the four to six kinds of client you actually deal with. Not names of
people — SHAPES. The examples the panel ships with are drawn from independent
Long Island agencies and look like this:

- the long-tenure personal lines household that has never once shopped you;
- the contractor who feels every general liability renewal in his margin;
- the restaurant owner who reads the premium line and nothing else;
- the landlord with several entities and one email address;
- the price shopper who arrives already holding a competitor's quote.

Yours may be different. Write yours.
