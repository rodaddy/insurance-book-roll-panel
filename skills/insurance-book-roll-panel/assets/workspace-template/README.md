# workspace-template

The skeleton of a panel workspace, copied out to the agency-owned location
recorded in `USER-CONFIG.md`. Nothing here holds agency data.

`PANEL-BRIEF.md` and `panel.json` define the panel; `book-profile.md` is what
the agency fills in about their book and what the seats are drawn from.
Subfolders: `inputs/` (the letter), `dossiers/`, `owner/`, `evidence/`, `runs/`,
and `calibration/`.
