# Source Notes

Record what each source actually supports. The strongest evidence an agency has
is its own history — the calls the front desk fielded after the last increase
letter, what leaving clients said on the way out, which cross-sell email got
replies. That beats anything external.

Never paste a policyholder's name, address, policy number, premium, or claim
detail into this file. Describe the pattern, not the person.

## Source entry template

- Source: <what it is — "front desk call log, week after the April renewal
  drop", "exit comments recorded by the account manager", "carrier rate filing
  notice", "our own last cross-sell email and its replies">
- Date observed: <date>
- Source type: <observed agency history, staff recollection, carrier or filing
  document, industry research, modeled assumption>
- Scope: <which part of the book, how many clients, what period>
- Supports: <the specific seat or roster observation this backs>
- Does not support: <what it cannot tell you>
- Reliability: <high, medium, or low, with the reason>

## A note on staff recollection

"Everybody called about that one" is worth recording and worth labeling
honestly. It is a real signal from people who were there, and it is not a count.
Write it as staff recollection with medium reliability, and let a later mailing
with an actual tally correct it.
