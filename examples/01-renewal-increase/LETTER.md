# The letter that went in

This is the letter the agency was about to mail to 380 personal lines
households. Identifiers were already replaced with fields before the run, which
is what the kit does to every letter.

---

Dear {{policyholder full name}},

Your homeowners policy with {{carrier name}} renews on
{{renewal effective date}}.

Your renewal premium for the coming term is {{renewal premium amount}}, which
reflects an increase of approximately 9 percent over your current premium. This
increase is the result of a statewide rate filing by {{carrier name}}, driven by
higher repair and rebuilding costs and by storm claims activity across the
region over the past two years. It is not related to your individual policy or
to any claim activity on your account.

Before sending this notice we reviewed your coverage and compared it against the
other carriers we represent. At your current coverage levels, this remains the
strongest option available to you through our office.

If you would like to review your coverage, your deductible, or your options,
please call our office and ask for your account manager.

Sincerely,

{{producer name}}
{{agency name}}
