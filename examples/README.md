# Worked examples

One complete run and one refusal-and-confirmation walkthrough on realistic
letters. Read these to see both the panel flow and the stop that protects it
before you risk your own mailing.

- `01-renewal-increase/` — a personal lines renewal letter carrying a 9 percent
  increase. Shows the common failure: the letter reads as a price-increase
  notice to most of the book, and the fix is not a better adjective.
- `02-cross-sell-refused/` — a cross-sell email that never gets washed, because
  the kit refuses it. Shows what a refusal looks like and how it is answered.

The first folder contains the letter, representative reactions, synthesis,
scorecard, and result. The second is deliberately a refusal walkthrough: it
shows the exact stop, the agency's answer, and the confirmation flag that lets
the unchanged letter proceed without the kit inventing missing facts.

## These are NOT for practising on

**The kit will refuse to run on these letters**, on purpose. They are an
illustration of somebody else's book, and a panel run against them would produce
a clean-looking report about nothing.

Your first real result has your own clients' shapes reacting to your own words.
That is the whole point, and it is why the install is not finished until one of
your letters has been through.

Once your own letter has been washed, running a demo against these is fine.

## What is fictional here

The agency, the letters, the reactions, and the outcomes are all constructed to
show the shape of a run. The CLIENT SHAPES the seats represent are drawn from
verified Long Island agency research; the reactions are illustrative and are not
evidence about any real policyholder. No real agency, person, policy, or premium
appears anywhere in these files.
